# Department AI Hands-on Meetup

## Summary

The Department AI Hands-on Meetup is a casual, hands-on practice session for students interested in AI. It will be held on October 16, 2026, from 18:30 to 20:00 (KST) in the Student Union meeting room. The session is free, with a capacity of 12 participants, and welcomes students who are completely new to AI practice. Please note that the entire meetup — both the guidance and the hands-on practice — is conducted in Korean. All times listed are in Korea Standard Time (Asia/Seoul, KST).

## Meeting Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Date & Time | October 16, 2026, 18:30–20:00 (KST) |
| Venue | Student Union meeting room |

| What to bring | Your own laptop and charger |
| Wi-Fi | Available at the venue (password/connection details are not provided in this announcement) |

| Who can join | Open to all students, including those new to AI practice |

## How to apply

To apply, leave your name in the department group chat — there is no separate application link. The application deadline is October 14, 2026, at 18:00 (KST).
